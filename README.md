# ProgressionLESS: Arsenal

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Overhauls weapon progression.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Arsenal adds up to 312 research projects depending on your mods. This fork groups them into at most 128, such as Swords, Revolvers, Gun turrets and Charge pistols. Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

Forked from [Progression: Arsenal](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3798837810) by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [The Progression Modpack](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585).
