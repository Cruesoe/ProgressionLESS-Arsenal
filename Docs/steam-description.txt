[h1]ProgressionLESS: Arsenal[/h1]
[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3798837810]Progression: Arsenal[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Overhauls weapon progression, so every weapon, turret and piece of combat gear unlocks in its proper tech era.
[*]Everything lives on its own Arsenal research tab, with tribal weapons on the VFE Tribals tab.
[*]Covers everything from clubs and slings to longswords, muskets, sniper rifles, warcaskets, turrets and spacer charge, laser and gauss weapons.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Arsenal adds up to [b]335 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 128[/b] weapon families:
[list]
[*][b]Tribal:[/b] Melee weapons, Tribal ranged weapons, Heavy tribal weapons, Megafauna bows
[*][b]Neolithic:[/b] Hatchets, Axes, Smithed blades, String instruments
[*][b]Medieval:[/b] Swords, Great swords, Battle axes and pikes, Great axes, War axes, Heavy maces, Two-handed and Military maces, Composite bows and crossbows, Longbows, Matchlock guns
[*][b]Industrial guns:[/b] Revolvers, Frontier guns, Blowback guns, Gas-operated SMGs, LMGs and carbines, Shotguns, Combat shotguns, Hunting rifles, Precision rifles, Marksman rifles, Sniper rifles
[*][b]Industrial heavy weapons:[/b] Grenade launchers, Gas grenade launchers, Mortar shells, Artillery, Heavy rocket launchers, Powered weapons, Multi-barrel guns, Non-lethal guns, Tox weapons
[*][b]Industrial defenses:[/b] Gun turrets, Concealed turrets, Non-lethal turrets, Multi-barrel turrets, Field fortifications
[*][b]Warcaskets:[/b] Warcasket sets, Heavy warcasket sets, Warcasket melee weapons, Warcasket guns
[*][b]Spacer:[/b] Charge pistols, Charge rifles and shotguns, Lance rifles, Spacer grenades, Charge turrets, Spacer IEDs, Impact, Ion, Laser, Salvaged laser, Gauss, Pulse, Phase and Fusion weapons, Rocketswarm launchers
[/list]
Weapons from different mods share a project when they share a family and a stage, so a weapon from one mod unlocks alongside its matching weapons from others.

Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before: flamer turrets still need biofuel refining, and each gas missile and gas grenade launcher still needs its disease research. Every industrial IED trap unlocks with IEDs.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey
[*]VE Weapons and its Makeshift, Frontier, Quickdraw, Tribal, Non-Lethal, Heavy Weapons, Laser and Coilgun add-ons
[*]VFE Tribals, Medieval 2, Classical, Settlers, Pirates, Empire, Deserters, Mechanoids and Security, and VRE Wasters
[*]Medieval Overhaul and House Roxmont
[*]Vanilla Arsenal, Spacer Arsenal, Impact, Pulse and Phase Weaponry, Ion Weaponry, Advanced Armaments, and the Laser, Security and Non-Lethal Re-Examined mods
[*]Alpha Genes, Alpha Biomes, Altered Carbon, Reinforced Mechanoid 2, Mechanoid Upgrades and Orbital Security
[*]Det's Xenotypes (Half-foot, Keshig, Venators, Pyrinth), Highborn, Rim-Elves, Big and Small, and Roo's Minotaur and Satyr
[*]Mythic Ages, Biomes! Mushkin, Rainbow Forest, Firefoam Things, Ancient Mining Industry, Reel's Turret Pipes, and As Above So Below 2
[*]Ushanka's Glittertech Expansion and Biological Warfare, Fireworks, and Bionic modularity
[*]Both Progression: Furniture and ProgressionLESS: Furniture
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Arsenal for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
